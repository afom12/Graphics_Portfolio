Abri Technologies

Client
Abri Technologies
Industry
Software & digital
Role
Lead designer

The brief

A new company building websites, mobile apps and custom business systems needed a brand that feels modern, confident and technical, and that stands out in a crowded tech market.

The solution

An angular "A" made of three sharp strokes that point upward and forward, like ideas taking off. A strong lime green on near-black gives the brand energy and makes it easy to recognise on any screen. The wide, clean wordmark keeps it professional.

Logo and wordmark · Color palette · Business cards · Social media launch carousel · 9:16 promo reel

Case study 02

Axum-X

Client
Axum-X (concept)
Industry
Luxury space travel
Project
InVision Africa

The brief

Ethiopia's first luxury space travel agency, offering tours to the moon, needed an identity that feels premium and timeless while honouring Ethiopian heritage.

The solution

An arch inspired by Aksumite architecture forms the letter "A". An orbit ring and a guiding star turn it into a gateway to space. Deep forest green, ivory and copper replace the usual "space blue" for a calm, high-end feel.

Deliverables

Logo (Illustrator, SVG) · Brand guidelines deck · Amharic social post · Stationery · Mockups

Finot Technologies

Client
Finot Technologies
Industry
Technology
Role
Designer

The project

A technology company needed a logo that feels trustworthy and forward-moving, plus business cards ready for professional printing.

The solution

A flowing "F" mark: a deep navy curve wraps around an orange path that rises and moves forward, showing growth and direction. The strong, wide FINOT wordmark gives stability, while the orange gradient adds warmth and energy. Artwork was prepared in Adobe Illustrator in CMYK for print.

Deliverables

Logo · Logo variations · Print-ready business cards (CMYK PDF)

LIYU Chicken

Client
LIYU Chicken (ልዩ ችክን)
Industry
Food & restaurant
Role

The brief

A new chicken restaurant brand needed a bold logo that stands out on packaging, signage and a brand-new TikTok account.

The solution

A confident rooster head in a circular badge. Red, white and golden yellow on black are warm, hungry colours that pop on any screen and can be read from across the street. The round shape works perfectly as a sticker, a sign and a profile picture.
